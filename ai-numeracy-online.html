Prompt 2

"Two shops offer rice at 5 kg for Rp72,000 and 8 kg for Rp112,000. Which is the better buy? Explain in a way suitable for a Kelas 6 learner."

Work it out yourself first, then check

5 kg at Rp72,000 is Rp14,400 per kg. 8 kg at Rp112,000 is Rp14,000 per kg. The 8 kg bag is the better buy, by Rp400 per kg.

Concept 2: Using generative AI for ideation

Draft a prompt, generate one problem, evaluate it

Review Module 1 and your earlier responses. Draft a prompt that gives the AI:

  • an overview of a typical class context for your PPG students;
  • what you consider constitutes an effective numeracy problem;
  • what opportunities there should be for students to engage with the problem (the general approach you want students to take).

If you have no preferred approach, you may align to Polya’s problem-solving steps: understand the problem, devise a plan, carry out the plan, and look back.

Use your prompt to generate one numeracy problem appropriate for a specific class.

Rate the generated problem from 1 (low) to 5 (high) on each criterion.
